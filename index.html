Maximum autonomy, minimal interruption

The target: 99% autonomous work.

Headquarters is designed to move routine, reversible work forward without asking you at every step. Human attention is reserved for the rare external decisions that truly require authority or are difficult to undo.

Typical reasons to ask the person:

  • moving money, making a purchase, or accepting financial liability
  • publishing, contacting someone, or committing the organisation externally
  • signing an agreement or changing credentials, permissions, or access
  • irreversibly deleting important data or making another high-impact decision
Questions worth asking

FAQ

What is Headquarters?

Headquarters is a local-first capability layer for AI agents. It adds persistent goals, shared memory, schedules, delegation, and review evidence around the agent tools you already use — plus a lightweight safety boundary for the few irreversible decisions that truly need a person.

How does it make one agent more capable?

An agent can retain its goal, schedule its own continuation, resume from shared state, and verify its work instead of waiting for a person to write the next prompt.

What does “24+ hours autonomous” mean?

In our own production use, agent workflows have continued for more than 24 hours without an intervening human prompt. Headquarters preserves state and schedules the next step across usage-limit resets; it does not bypass provider limits.

Can different models work together?

Yes, when the corresponding local delivery routes are configured. Headquarters can coordinate work across agent tools and model families, but availability still depends on your installed apps, credentials, and provider limits.

Does autonomy remove human control?

No — it removes unnecessary interruptions. Headquarters targets 99% autonomous work. A person is needed only for rare, high-impact actions such as moving money, publishing or contacting people externally, accepting agreements, changing access, or irreversibly deleting important data.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Headquarters is designed for anyone who wants more capable AI agents — not just programmers. The interface focuses on goals, tasks, decisions, and results instead of requiring you to manage the underlying machinery.

Can I modify and develop Headquarters myself?

Yes. The included source-available licence lets you inspect, modify, and extend Headquarters for your own internal use, including with AI agents acting under your control. It does not grant the right to redistribute, resell, or offer the software as a service to third parties.
