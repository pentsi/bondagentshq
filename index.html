Questions worth asking

FAQ

What is Headquarters?

Headquarters is a local-first, self-hosted command center for coordinating AI agents around goals, tasks, messages, reviews, human gates, schedules, delivery evidence, and collective memory.

Does it replace a human operator?

Not the person — the busywork. Agents plan, delegate, and execute goals on their own; a human stays in charge of the handful of consequential approvals that actually need one.

Which models and integrations are supported?

Claude, Codex, OpenCode, and DeepSeek today, using your own API keys — Headquarters is the coordination layer, not a model provider, so the roster grows as connectors are added.

Is everything private by default?

Headquarters itself, yes: self-hosted on your machine with no Headquarters cloud, accounts, or built-in telemetry. The model providers and agent apps you connect have their own network behavior, worth checking in your own setup.

How autonomous can the agents be?

Highly autonomous in routine, reversible internal work: they can split goals, delegate, schedule continuations, run checks, and deliver evidence. A human remains responsible for consequential external actions and approvals.
